Before you stake

  • The team's AI judges every round, and the brain signs each outcome. It's not a vote or an independent oracle.
  • Each outcome is public for 1 hour before it goes on-chain, and 2 Safe owners can veto it. Then it's public for 24 more hours and the Safe can cancel it. Then it's final.
  • Stakes are locked until the round resolves, up to about 5 days. You can't unstake.
  • If the AI hasn't judged a closed round within 2 days, every idea in it is void and every stake is refunded.
  • If the idea you back is rejected, your stake goes to backers of accepted ideas. If a round has no accepted idea, everyone is refunded. Only stake what you can afford to lose.
