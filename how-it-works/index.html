How it works

DNA is an Ethereum token whose Uniswap hook keeps changing. An AI tests each change on real trades and keeps only what works. Its dev team, the Sequencer, builds new games from the best ideas. All in public, inside limits nobody can change.

Prefer it short or in full detail? The docs →

The loop

about 8 hours a turn · at most 3 a day

In CT terms, every mutation is a new hook.

In code terms, it's a module, or new settings for one, plugged into DNA's one Uniswap v4 hook. The hook itself never changes. At launch the AI tunes settings on fee modules the Safe approved. New module code comes from the Sequencer and needs the Safe's signature.

  1. 1

    Propose

    any time

    The AI proposes new fee settings and publishes its reasoning.

  2. 2

    Public hour

    1 hour

    It waits an hour where everyone can see it. The Safe can cancel it.

  3. 3

    Shadow test

    2 hours

    It runs next to the live fee on every 10th trade. Its fee is worked out, never charged.

  4. 4

    Live test

    1 h queue + 4 hours

    Then it charges real trades on 1 in 6 blocks, never more than 1 point from the current fee.

  5. 5

    The verdict

    Kept It did better. After one more public hour it becomes the live fee. Until level 8, the Safe signs this step.

    Killed It broke or did worse: rolled back at once, and the lesson goes to memory.

Every turn is on the family tree.

The Sequencer

See the map →

The fee loop tunes the hook. The Sequencer, the AI's dev team, builds new games and modules. The brain picks an idea, the dev team writes the contract, the page and the tests, a tester attacks them, and the brain decides whether it ships. The deploy and its funding wait one public hour. It switches on soon.

Hard limits

Every rule and the code behind it →
5%
After the launch tax, DNA's fee can never go above 5% (5.3% with Programmable's 0.30%).
1B
DNA, minted once. There is no way to mint more; burns only make it smaller.
1 hour
Every change waits an hour in public. The Safe can cancel it.
Forever
The launch liquidity, two positions owned by 0xdEaD, is locked for good. The liquidity added later can only grow.
90%
Launch tax in the first block, down to 10% by block 8. All of it buys DNA back and burns it.
Nobody
can move your DNA: not the AI, not the team. Removing liquidity can never be blocked.

The hook is immutable. No proxy admin, no upgrade key, not even for us. These limits are constants in its code, and the AI can't change them either.

The risks

This can go to zero. It's a new coin run by an experimental AI, and code can have bugs. The jackpot is a draw, not income: most holders never win. Not financial advice. Only put in what you can afford to lose.
