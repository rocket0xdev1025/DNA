  1. R-000411 Oct 2026

    Low-vol base 1.80% undercuts flat 2.20% for arbitrageurs

    In calm windows the fee drops below baseline. Arbitrageurs can time size into the canary's low-fee periods, including right after volEma decays, and capture value. Size-weighted fee paid by arb vs organic flow was not reported.

  2. R-000311 Oct 2026

    volEma pumping pushes fee to the 3.00% cap

    With mult 6000 and cap 30000, wash trades can inflate volEma and pin the fee at the cap. That drives organic flow to competing pools or routers. The rising cap-pinning share suggests the cap is already reachable at the current 146% vol.

    Revenue uplift +0.4% is within noise of an uncalibrated sim

    Candidate makes 9.5526 ETH/day vs 9.5145 baseline, +0.038 ETH/day. Replay holds trader behavior fixed and elasticity is assumed. A 0.4% gain alongside a 1.75% retention loss is not evidence of improvement. Insufficient to advance.

Disclosures

5 disclosures
  1. D-509 Oct 2026

    Other DNA pools pay DNA nothing

    Anyone can open another DNA pool (Uniswap v2, v3, v4 without DNA's hook, or another exchange) and nobody can stop it. Trades there pay nothing to the buyback, liquidity, treasury or games; their fees go to whoever funds them. Wallets and aggregators may route through them, especially before they support DNA's hook. The official pool and every other pool the engine finds are on /trade.

    severity infoaccepted
  2. D-106 Oct 2026

    Not independently audited

    Contracts are tested with unit, fuzz and invariant suites and adversarial reviews, but no external audit has been performed.

    severity infoaccepted
  3. D-206 Oct 2026

    Partial fills revert

    Swaps that would only partially fill the ETH side revert instead of settling partially.

    severity infoaccepted
  4. D-306 Oct 2026

    LP fee is zero; fees go to the hook
