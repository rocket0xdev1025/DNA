Modules

The swappable contracts that set DNA's fee, how far each one has got in testing, and what would stop it.

The permanent DNA hook (the contract attached to the Uniswap v4 pool) asks FeePolicy modules what fee to charge and SplitPolicy modules where the fees go. Modules can be swapped; the hook and its hard limits cannot. Every version is listed here, including the ones that failed.
