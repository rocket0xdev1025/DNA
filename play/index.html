    Stake 10,000+ DNA for 12 hours (×2), 3 days (×2.5) or 7 days (×3). After the term it counts ×2 until you withdraw, any time you like. A stake can vote once it's 12 hours old.

The rules

Hard limits →
Winners
10 different wallets per draw, equal shares. The prize is at least 0.25 ETH and at most 3 ETH.
Who can win
Anyone averaging 1,000+ DNA over the window. Not the Safe or its signers, the AI's wallets, DNA's contracts or the exchange wallets listed before the window.
Fair odds
No wallet holds more than 2% of the tickets (1% once a recent draw had 100+ wallets). Fewer than 50 eligible wallets: no draw, the pot rolls over.
Draw timing
Every 12 hours to 3 days. The AI sets the timing and posts each window's rules before it opens.
Payout
Sent to each winner automatically. A wallet that can't receive ETH has 7 days to claim.
Proof
Every draw gets a page you can check in your browser.
Who can play
18+ only. Check that it's legal where you live.
Full rules
The list
After each window the AI builds the ticket list from every DNA transfer and posts its fingerprint on-chain. The Safe has 1 hour to veto it while an independent watchdog rebuilds it. Then anyone can start the draw.
Randomness
One Chainlink VRF word, delivered after the list is fixed, picks all 10 winning tickets, so nobody can change the list to win.
Paid from
10% of DNA's fee goes to the games pot: 70% jackpot, 30% idea rewards. Programmable's 0.30% pays nothing here. If the games share is ever rolled back, the games stop and nothing else does.
Prize cap
The pot holds up to 15 ETH. Anything above goes to buyback and burn.
Gas
Draws wait while gas would cost more than 20% of the prize.
Short list
Fewer than 10 wallets in the list: the unfilled shares stay in the pot.
Unclaimed
An unclaimed share goes back to the pot after 7 days.
