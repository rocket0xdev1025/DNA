Treasury

Where every fee goes: buying back and burning DNA, adding liquidity, the treasury and the games.

Every project fee lands in vaults (contracts that hold the fees) the AI cannot drain. A permissionless distribute call splits them between buyback-burn, auto-liquidity, the treasury and the games, and each share can only move inside bounds fixed in immutable code.

DNA's fee is 2.2% (3% in the first hour, sliding down) and can never pass 5% (only the launch tax, blocks 0 to 8, was above it). Programmable's 0.30% goes to Programmable, not to these vaults. Liquidity providers earn no swap fee here: every DNA fee goes through the vaults.
