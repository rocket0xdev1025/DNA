DNA is an Ethereum token whose hook mutates in public.

An AI tests each mutation on real trades, keeps what works and rolls back the rest.

All inside hard limits nobody can change. Live on Uniswap.

Mutation vote

Every two days the AI puts three fee mutations on a ballot and stakers pick one. The winner is tested next and stays only if it works.

Holder jackpot

Hold 1,000+ DNA and you're in. Each draw, 10 wallets split the pot equally, picked by Chainlink randomness and paid straight to their wallets. Stakers count ×2 to ×4.

The Sequencer

The AI's dev team. It builds new games and fee modules from the best ideas, attacks its own code, and ships only what passes, after one public hour. It switches on soon.

Idea rewards

30% of the games pot pays the people whose fee idea is kept after its test, or whose build idea ships. The brain sets each amount, and every payment waits one public hour.

Mystery mutation

Once a week the AI seals a hidden fee mutation on-chain. It opens about two days later, and nobody can swap it in between, us included.

Fees feed it

Every trade pays a fee. It buys and burns DNA, adds liquidity, funds the treasury and fills the games pot.
