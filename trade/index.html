Buy or sell DNA in its own pool: the one whose fees fund the buyback, liquidity and the games.

The swap goes from your wallet straight to Uniswap's Universal Router and into DNA's pool, nothing in between. Wallets and aggregators may not find this pool for the first days, until they add support for its hook.

Anyone can open another DNA pool, on Uniswap or elsewhere, and nobody can stop it. Trades there pay nothing to DNA: no buyback, no liquidity, no jackpot. Their fees go to whoever put money in them. The official pool is the one above. We list the others here so you can tell them apart, and never link to them.
